# Exquisite Core

Hi friend. I have an idea…

Remember the game exquisite corpse? Each person draws a portion of a body and then folds the paper over,
and the next person has to work from just the tiny stubs of lines that wrap over the fold?

![Ex](/images/exquisite-corpse.png)

I want to do that, but with software. Here's the premise:

- We'll get 5-10 people in a room. Some will be developers, some not.
- At any moment during the night, one or another person's name will be called. When that happens, a 60 second timer starts.
- During those 60 seconds, that person can browse the current state of our webapp. The first time, they're going in completely blind. Subsequent times they're going in only knowing what they saw during the last round.
- After 60 seconds, you have 60 seconds to write **one** AI prompt and send it.

We repeat that loop for as many iterations as makes sense, and then demo the app at the end of the night.

The vibe should be: exquisite corpse meets musical chairs meets whisper down the lane, but with nerds and the web.

I'm gonna try to organize a small group sometime in November or December, and if it goes well potentially scale it up to a full PHP×Philly meetup event in early 2027. If you want to be part of that first round, let me know!
